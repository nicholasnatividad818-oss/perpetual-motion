# perpetual-motion

3D visualization of the digital-root doubling wheel **1-2-4-8-7-5** and the numbers that refuse it: **3, 6, and 9**.

Live: after Pages or Vercel lights, open the deployed URL. Locally: open `index.html`.

- Cyan torus: motion (powers of two, reduced to one digit)
- Ember icosahedra: 3 and 6, orbiting off the wheel
- Gold octahedron: 9 on the still axis

This is modular arithmetic made visible, not a free-energy machine.

Repo: https://github.com/nicholasnatividad818-oss/perpetual-motion
